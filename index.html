Sweet potatoes · the heart of our farm

Vitamin A

Eyes · Skin · Immunity

The orange colour comes from beta-carotene, which your body turns into vitamin A. One medium sweet potato gives you more than a full day's vitamin A. That supports good eyesight, healthy skin and a strong immune system.

Fibre & slow energy

Digestion · Steady blood sugar

Sweet potatoes are full of fibre, especially with the skin on. Fibre feeds healthy gut bacteria and makes the energy last. Boiled sweet potatoes have a lower glycaemic index than baked ones.

Potassium & antioxidants

Heart · Muscles

They are a good source of potassium, vitamin C, vitamin B6 and manganese. Their antioxidants help protect your cells from everyday wear and tear.

Why we farm the slow way

What “bio” means on our farm

Bio means organic. We grow food the way our grandparents did, and we take care of the soil, the water and the people who eat what we grow.

01

No synthetic sprays

We use no synthetic pesticides or herbicides. That means fewer chemical residues on your plate, and on ours.

02

Living soil

We use compost and manure, rotate our crops and keep the soil covered. Healthy soil grows healthy plants.

03

Picked ripe

Our produce isn't shipped across continents, so we can let it ripen fully. Ripe food tastes better.

04

Good for the valley

No chemical run-off in the Alazani Valley, and more room for bees, birds and earthworms.
